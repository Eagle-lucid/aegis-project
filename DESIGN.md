# Aegis Design Rationale

## Core Philosophy: Cinematic Sovereignty

The design must feel like a fusion of a king's war room and a starship bridge. It is dark, regal, and futuristic. Every element must serve clarity or command.

## Color Palette

- **Primary Background:** `#0D1117` (Deep Abyss)
- **Primary Action:** `#10B981` (Sovereign Emerald)
- **Information:** `#22D3EE` (Oracle Cyan)
- **Highlight:** `#8B5CF6` (Royal Violet)

## Key Interactions & Animations

1.  **Hero Section:** Throne descends on Y-axis, rotates to face user. H1 text reveals character-by-character with a "focus" effect.
2.  **The Aegis Catalyst:** The "Before-After-Bridge" section uses a scroll-triggered wave transformation emanating from a central Catalyst icon.
3.  **Orbit Features:** Four panels orbit a central point. On hover, one panel moves forward while the others recede.

## Typography

- **Display Font:** Clash Display / Unbounded (for H1, "The Summoning")
- **Body Font:** Inter / System UI (for all other text)
